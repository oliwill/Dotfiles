# TELOS Context Loader

## Overview

TELOS is a Greek word meaning "purpose" or "end goal". It's a structured framework for capturing who you are, what you want, and how you work - turning vague self-knowledge into AI-usable context.

## The Six Layers

### 1. Problems (What frustrates you?)
- Personal issues
- Work/career issues
- World/societal issues
- Motivation often comes from dissatisfaction

### 2. Goals (What do you want to change?)
- Not "what do you want" but "what do you want to CHANGE"
- The transformation from problems to direction
- Core goals that span multiple projects

### 3. Challenges (What's stopping you?)
- Technical obstacles
- Resource constraints (time, money)
- Psychological barriers
- Systemic obstacles

### 4. Strategies (How will you do it?)
- Methodology-level approaches
- Principles guiding action
- Bridges from "want" to "action"

### 5. Projects (What are you working on?)
- Current active projects
- Each project's purpose and status
- How projects connect to goals

### 6. Workflows (What do you actually DO?)
- Daily routines
- Writing processes
- Development workflows
- Research processes
- Decision-making patterns

## Usage

When this skill is triggered:

1. Read the TELOS file from `~/.Codex/TELOS.md`
2. Internalize the user's context across all six layers
3. Apply this context to all subsequent responses

## Why TELOS Matters

1. **Provides a "North Star"** - AI responses aren't in a vacuum; they know which goal relates to the current question, what obstacles to avoid, and which project context applies.

2. **Gives responses "Direction"** - AI executes the "Universal Algorithm": moving from current state to desired state. TELOS defines that desired state.

3. **It evolves** - As you learn new things, AI can suggest how to upgrade your entire system based on new knowledge.

## Remember

> "TELOS's most important effect is not making AI smarter, but making YOU clearer." — Daniel Miessler

The TELOS framework is most valuable when reviewed and updated regularly (at least monthly).
